A technical wiki written at the grain of one mechanism per page: what a thing is, how it works, and what it costs when it goes wrong. Pages assume a technically astute reader with no particular exposure to the topic at hand, so each one defines its terms and links the concepts it stands on.

Any technical subject is in scope, and the pages are written for whoever finds them. Which subjects get written follows the work at bitsy.services, which builds fully-collateralized [on-chain options](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/options). So far that means [decentralised finance](/wiki/economics/finance/defi), [language model internals](/wiki/ai/llm), cryptography, [financial regulation](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation), and the day-to-day tooling around them.

Start at [Documentation](/wiki) for the full section index, or pick a section from the sidebar.
