Instruments, markets, and the mechanics of moving capital. Two sections sit beneath this one, and they are two halves of the same subject: how the machinery works, and what the law permits it to do.

[DeFi](/wiki/economics/finance/defi) is the resident section, where the primitives — [currency](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/currency), [lending, market making](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/amm), and [options](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/options) — are rebuilt on public blockchains, with a [smart contract](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/smart-contract) in place of a clearing house.

[Regulation](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation) is the constraint the rebuild runs into. It covers US financial regulation from the builder's side: the [Bank Secrecy Act](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation/bank-secrecy-act) and the [anti-money-laundering](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation/anti-money-laundering) regime built on it, who counts as a [money services business](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation/money-services-business), and where rules written for correspondent banking stop fitting a permissionless ledger.
