Instruments, markets, and the mechanics of moving capital. The resident section is [DeFi](/wiki/economics/finance/defi), where those same primitives — [currency](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/currency), [lending, market making](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/amm), and [options](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/options) — are rebuilt on public blockchains, with a [smart contract](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/smart-contract) in place of a clearing house.
