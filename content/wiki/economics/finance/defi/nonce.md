---
title: "Nonce"
weight: 30
---

In [blockchain](/wiki/economics/finance/defi/blockchain) networks like Ethereum, a nonce is a per-account transaction counter that increments with each transaction sent. It serves two purposes: preventing replay attacks (the same signed transaction can't be submitted twice) and ordering transactions from the same sender.
