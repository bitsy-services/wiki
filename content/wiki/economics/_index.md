How value gets created, priced, and moved. The section leads with [finance](/wiki/economics/finance) — instruments, markets, and the machinery that settles a trade, split between the [DeFi](/wiki/economics/finance/defi) rebuild of that machinery, the [payments](/wiki/economics/finance/payments) rails it is measured against, the [regulation](/wiki/economics/finance/regulation) both have to survive, and the [fraud](/wiki/economics/finance/fraud) that irreversible settlement makes profitable — and is scoped to grow into the wider subject: monetary systems, incentives and mechanism design, and the economics of running computation at scale.
