An automated market maker (AMM) is a type of [decentralized exchange](/wiki/defi/dex) that uses a mathematical formula — rather than an order book — to price trades. Traders swap against a [liquidity pool](/wiki/defi/liquidity-pool) instead of matching with a counterparty.

The most common AMM formula is the constant product (`x · y = k`), used by [Uniswap](/wiki/defi/uniswap) V2. Other designs include constant-sum, constant-mean (Balancer), and concentrated-liquidity models (Uniswap V3/V4).

AMMs made on-chain trading practical by removing the need for active market makers and order book infrastructure that doesn't map well to blockchain constraints.
