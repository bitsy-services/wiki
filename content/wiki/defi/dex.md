---

A decentralized exchange (DEX) is a marketplace for trading tokens that runs entirely on-chain, without a central operator holding custody of user funds. Trades settle directly between the user's wallet and a smart contract.

DEXs come in several flavours: [AMM](/wiki/defi/amm)-based (Uniswap, Curve, Balancer), order-book-based (dYdX, Serum), and intent-based systems (UniswapX, CoW Swap) where off-chain solvers compete to fill orders.
