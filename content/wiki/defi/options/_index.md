An **option** is a contract granting the right -- but not the obligation -- to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price. This section covers options from first principles up through the fully-collateralized, on-chain derivatives Bitsy builds: [cash-backed synthetic options](/wiki/defi/options/cash-backed-synthetic-option).

The pages divide into four layers, each building on the one before.

## Fundamentals

The vocabulary every other page assumes: the [option type](/wiki/defi/options/option-type) distinction ([call](/wiki/defi/options/call-option) vs. [put](/wiki/defi/options/put-option)), the [strike price](/wiki/defi/options/strike-price), and the pricing sensitivities -- [implied volatility](/wiki/defi/options/implied-volatility) and the [option Greeks](/wiki/defi/options/option-greeks). [Volatility](/wiki/defi/volatility) itself is treated as a general DeFi concept and lives at the DeFi top level.

## Spreads and Strategies

Multi-leg structures that combine options to shape a payoff curve: the [option spread](/wiki/defi/options/option-spread) in general, the [vertical spread](/wiki/defi/options/vertical-spread) specifically, the expiry-free [perpetual option](/wiki/defi/options/perpetual-option), the notion of a [risk-defined strategy](/wiki/defi/options/risk-defined-strategy), and how all of these can be [emulated with DeFi primitives](/wiki/defi/options/emulating-option-strategies) such as concentrated [liquidity-pool](/wiki/defi/liquidity-pool) positions.

## Directional Bets

The user-facing products: [bull bets](/wiki/defi/options/bull-bet) and [bear bets](/wiki/defi/options/bear-bet), and the four spreads they package -- [bull call](/wiki/defi/options/bull-call-spread), [bull put](/wiki/defi/options/bull-put-spread), [bear call](/wiki/defi/options/bear-call-spread), and [bear put](/wiki/defi/options/bear-put-spread).

## Cash-Backed Synthetic Options

Bitsy's core derivative: the [cash-backed synthetic option](/wiki/defi/options/cash-backed-synthetic-option) (CBSO), its [smart-contract design](/wiki/defi/options/cash-backed-synthetic-options-design), and the [innovative claims](/wiki/defi/options/innovative-claims-cbso) it makes -- full collateralization, no margin calls, and settlement in volatile currencies.
