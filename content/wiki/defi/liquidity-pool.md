---

A liquidity pool is a smart contract that holds reserves of two or more tokens and allows anyone to trade against those reserves according to a deterministic pricing algorithm. Pools are the core primitive of [AMM](/wiki/defi/amm)-based [decentralized exchanges](/wiki/defi/dex).

Liquidity providers (LPs) deposit tokens into the pool and receive a proportional share of the trading fees collected. In return, they take on the risk of [impermanent loss](/wiki/defi/impermanent-loss).
