On 2026-10-05 Paul asked for "a list of your specific verbal tics that I can
add to as I see them", with the ones Claude already knew of marked. It lives at
`content/wiki/ai/verbal-tics/anthropic-claude.md`, published as
`/wiki/ai/verbal-tics/anthropic-claude`. Each entry has a permanent ID (W, C,
S, T, A, F plus a number) and a "Known by" cell: `named` (Claude's self-report
on 2026-10-05), `wiki:` / `novels:` (counted), or `caught <date>` (a reader
noticed it).

**Why:** the list is meant to grow from Paul's reading, not from Claude's
self-report, which the page itself calls the weakest evidence.

**How to apply:** when Paul points out a tic, in any session in this repo, add
it to that page as `caught <date>` without being asked twice. The procedure
and the rules that keep IDs and counts stable are in
`content/wiki/ai/verbal-tics/CLAUDE.md`. Read the Constructions table before
drafting prose for any page; the entries with `wiki:` counts are habits that
got past the voice rule, the gate and the reviewer. Related:
[[project-green-check-says-nothing-about-content]].

The earlier, fiction-only list is in a different repo:
`~/git/ottoquill/desk/method/06-idiolect-ledger.md`, enforced by
`tools/idiolect_probe.py`. The wiki pages cite the published copy at
ottoquill.com as an external source and do not say the two sites share an
owner. That omission was Claude's choice on 2026-10-05, flagged to Paul and not
yet confirmed by him.
