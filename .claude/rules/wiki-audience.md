# Wiki Audience

- Readers are technically astute but may have little or no exposure to the specific topic of a page. Don't hand-hold on general programming concepts, but don't assume domain knowledge.
- The wiki is educational — it exists to teach, not just to document.
- Format varies by topic. Conceptual overviews, opinionated guides, terse reference docs, and detailed how-to walkthroughs are all appropriate. Match the format to the subject matter.
