# Wiki Scope

A general-purpose technical wiki, published at wiki.bitsy.services for whoever
finds it. Any technical subject is in scope. Which subjects actually get written
follows the work at bitsy.services, but that is a statement about supply, not
about eligibility.

- No topic is disqualified for being unrelated to Bitsy. A subject with no
  existing home gets a new section, not a justification. That is a statement
  about eligibility only: `wiki-taxonomy.md` decides what the section is called
  and at which level it sits, and a new *top-level* section is a shape change
  worth confirming before it lands.
- Bitsy-specific pages are written the same way as everything else: explain the
  mechanism, not the company. A reader who has never heard of Bitsy should still
  get something out of the page.
- The grain is fine — one mechanism to a page. When a subject stands on its own,
  prefer a new page and a link over another `##` on an existing page.
- Assume the reader arrived from a search engine on that one page. That is what
  makes the per-page acronym rule in `wiki-content.md` and the stub rule in
  `wiki-linking.md` load-bearing rather than pedantic: nothing carries over from
  a page they did not read.
