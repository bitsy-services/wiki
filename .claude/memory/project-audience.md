name: Wiki audience and tone
description: Target audience profile and content style guidelines for the Bitsy Services Wiki
type: project
---

**Audience**: Technically astute readers who may have little or no exposure to the specific topic of a given page. Don't hand-hold on general programming concepts, but don't assume domain knowledge.

**Goal**: Educational — the wiki exists to teach, not just to document.

**Tone and format**: Varies by topic. All of the following are appropriate depending on the page:
- Conceptual overviews (high-level, explanatory)
- Opinionated guides
- Terse reference docs
- Detailed how-to walkthroughs (nitty-gritty task-specific)

Match the format to the subject matter. Some pages will be deeply practical; others will be purely conceptual.

**How to apply:** When drafting a page, pick the format that fits the topic. Always explain domain-specific terms and concepts on first use (per the existing wiki-content rule about linking terms). Write for someone smart who just hasn't seen this particular thing before.
