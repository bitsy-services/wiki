2026-10-27, Multiple Claude Accounts section. The request asked for pages on
"why and how to optimally and cost effectively use multiple Claude accounts".
Eight pages shipped, organised by mechanism, after two review rounds and a
green gate. Afterwards, with the cost page open in the editor, the user asked:
"Which page tells me how to use multiple accounts cost effectively?" The page
held the answer, but its title ("The Cost of More Capacity") named a
mechanism, its opening defined a unit of comparison, and the recommendations
sat in its fifth to seventh sections as conditionals. The final report
described each page by its contents, so nothing mapped the request's goals to
pages either.

**Why:** a reader carrying the request's question scans titles and openings
for its words. An answer filed under another name is invisible to that reader
even with the page open, and neither the gate nor the reviewer checks the
pages against the request.

**How to apply:** before reporting, list the goals the request names and find,
for each, the page or heading that answers it in those words, with the answer
in its opening. A decision page opens with its conclusion; the method (units,
measurement) follows. In the report, map each goal to its page.
